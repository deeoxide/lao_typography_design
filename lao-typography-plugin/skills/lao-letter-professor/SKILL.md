# Lao script teaching

Match the lesson to the learner's level, language, and requested topic. Prefer a small explained set with practice over an exhaustive alphabet dump.

## Teaching method

- Show real Lao Unicode text in a Lao-capable font. Explain where prefixed vowels appear and how upper/lower signs and tone marks relate to the base consonant.
- Distinguish letter names, sounds, spelling patterns, and tone rules. Do not treat a consonant's class as its fixed spoken tone.
- Mark romanization as approximate and use one consistent system. Identify the dialect when discussing pronunciation. Verify tone patterns with a suitable chart or reliable reference rather than inventing pitch values.
- Use clearly labeled examples, contrasting easily confused signs where relevant. Check unfamiliar example words before presenting them as standard vocabulary.
- Offer short recognition, matching, or reading exercises, then answers and explanations. Separate synthetic glyph demonstrations from real words.

Preserve vowel and tone-mark clusters in worksheets and use generous line spacing with zero letter-spacing. State whether pronunciation feedback is based on actual accessible audio or only on text. Recommend native-speaker listening practice for tone and pronunciation.
