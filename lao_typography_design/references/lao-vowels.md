# Topic: Lao vowels (ສະຫຼະ)

Lao has about **28 vowel forms**, including diphthongs. Vowels are not written as separate letters in a line. They are signs attached to a consonant. In every chart below, **ກ** stands for "any consonant".

## 1. Four positions

A vowel can be written in one of four places around its consonant:

| Position | Vowel signs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Before (prefixed) | ເ ແ ໂ ໄ ໃ | ເກ, ແກ, ໂກ, ໄກ, ໃກ |
| After (to the right) | ະ າ ຳ | ກະ, ກາ, ກຳ |
| Above | ິ ີ ຶ ື ັ ົ | ກິ, ກີ, ກຶ, ກື, ກັ, ກົ |
| Below | ຸ ູ | ກຸ, ກູ |

- The five **prefixed vowels** (ເ ແ ໂ ໄ ໃ) are written before the consonant but pronounced after it. For example, ໄກ່ is read "kai", not "ai-k".
- Some vowels combine two parts around the consonant, such as ເ-ະ, ເ-ືອ, ເ-ົາ.
- A tone mark sits above the consonant or above the vowel sign when one is there, for example ກິ່ນ.

## 2. Short and long pairs

Most Lao vowels come in **short / long pairs**. The length changes both the sound and the tone behavior of the syllable.

| Short | Long | IPA (short / long) | Example words |
|---|---|---|---|
| ກະ | ກາ | a / aa | ຕາ (eye), ປາ (fish), ຊ້າງ (elephant) |
| ກິ | ກີ | i / ii | ລີງ (monkey), ວີ (fan) |
| ກຶ | ກື | ɯ / ɯɯ | |
| ກຸ | ກູ | u / uu | ຍຸງ (mosquito), ທຸງ (flag), ງູ (snake), ພູ (mountain) |
| ເກະ | ເກ | e / ee | ເດັກ (child) |
| ແກະ | ແກ | ɛ / ɛɛ | ແບ້ (goat), ແມວ (cat) |
| ໂກະ | ໂກ | o / oo | ໂອ (bowl) |
| ເກາະ | ກໍ | ɔ / ɔɔ | ຈອກ (cup) |
| ເກິ | ເກີ | ɤ / ɤɤ | ເຜິ້ງ (bee) |

Notes on the written forms:

- When the short vowel has a **final consonant**, it is usually written with a sign above, not with ະ. For example, short "a" becomes **ັ** (ກັນ), and short "o" becomes **ົ** (ນົກ, ຖົງ, ຝົນ, ລົດ).
- ະ is used when the syllable has **no final consonant**.
- Long "ɔɔ" is written **ໍ** when there is no final consonant (ກໍ), and with **ອ** when there is a final consonant (ຈອກ).

## 3. Diphthongs and other compound vowels

| Vowel | Sound (IPA) | Notes and examples |
|---|---|---|
| ເ-ຍ | ia | no final consonant |
| ກຽ + final | ia | with a final consonant, ຽ is used (for example ສຽງ, "sound") |
| ເ-ືອ | ɯa | ເສືອ (tiger), ເຮືອນ (house) |
| -ົວ | ua | no final consonant |
| ກວ + final | ua | with a final consonant, ວ is used |
| ໄ- | ai | ໄຟ (fire), ໄກ່ (chicken), ໄຂ່ (egg) |
| ໃ- | ai | same sound as ໄ-, used in a limited set of words; spell each word as it is written |
| ເ-ົາ | ao | |
| -ຳ | am | ກຳ |

Short versions of ia, ɯa, and ua exist (written with a short mark and final), so check the exact form in a reliable chart before teaching or typesetting.

## 4. The carrier ອ

The letter **ອ** is the mid-class "carrier" consonant. When a syllable has a vowel but **no starting consonant**, ອ stands in for it:

ອະ, ອາ, ອິ, ອີ, ອຶ, ອື, ອຸ, ອູ, ເອ, ແອ, ໂອ, ອໍ, ເອີ, ໄອ, ເອົາ, ອຳ

ອ is also used as a vowel (long ɔɔ) with a final consonant, as in ຈອກ. Its role depends on its position in the syllable.

## 5. Syllable types and tone

The vowel length and the final consonant decide whether a syllable is **live** (ຄຳເປັນ) or **dead** (ຄຳຕາຍ), and this changes the tone:

- **Live:** a long vowel, or a vowel followed by a sonorant final (ງ ນ ມ ຍ ວ).
- **Dead:** a short vowel with no final, or a vowel followed by a stop final (ກ ດ ບ).

Tone = consonant class + syllable type + tone mark. Use a tone chart for exact pitches before stating a tone.

## 6. Typesetting and AI notes

- **Unicode order:** prefixed vowels (ເ ແ ໂ ໄ ໃ, U+0EC0–0EC4) come first in the text and appear before the consonant. Above and below signs follow the consonant. A tone mark follows the vowel sign.
- **Fonts:** use a Lao font with proper OpenType shaping (default: Noto Sans Lao). Without shaping, vowels and marks float or overlap.
- **Line height:** keep 1.5 or more. Stacked vowel signs and tone marks need vertical space.
- **No letter-spacing.** It pulls the combining signs off their consonant.
- **Do not cut in the middle of a syllable.** Never split a vowel or tone mark from its consonant when truncating or wrapping.
- **Check look-alikes:** ຸ vs ູ, ິ vs ີ, ຶ vs ື differ by a small stroke or length mark. Proofread them at normal size.

## 7. Common mistakes

- Reading ເ ແ ໂ ໄ ໃ in written order. They are read after the consonant.
- Ignoring vowel length. Short and long forms are different words.
- Using ະ with a final consonant, or ັ / ົ with no final.
- Writing ວ and ອ in the wrong role (consonant vs vowel).
- Mixing in Thai vowel signs. Lao and Thai look similar but use different codepoints.

## 8. Verify before publishing

Vowel spelling rules have many exceptions, especially for ໃ- words and short diphthongs. Please have a native speaker confirm the tables above, and replace example words with ones from your own teaching list if needed.
